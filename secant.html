Secant Method Visualizer

By drawing a secant line between points (x_{i-1}, f(x_{i-1})) and (x_i, f(x_i)), the next root estimate is its x-intercept: x_{i+1} = x_i - f(x_i) \cdot \frac{x_i - x_{i-1}}{f(x_i) - f(x_{i-1})}. No analytical derivatives required, converging with superlinear speed φ ≈ 1.618!

💡 Method Comparison

vs. False Position: False Position locks onto one side if curvature is one-sided, slowing to linear convergence. Secant always discards the older point, yielding superlinear convergence (φ ≈ 1.618).

vs. Newton-Raphson: Newton-Raphson requires evaluating both f(x) and f'(x) (2 evaluations/step). Secant requires only one new evaluation of f(x) per iteration, often winning in computational efficiency!
