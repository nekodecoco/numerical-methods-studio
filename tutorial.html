Demystifying the Taylor Series

How does a computer calculate sin(0.42) or e^1.7? Computers cannot directly calculate transcendent curves — they only add and multiply. Taylor polynomials are the bridge that transforms transcendent curves into simple polynomial addition and multiplication!

💡 1. The Core Philosophy

Matching Derivatives = Matching Reality

Imagine tracking a drone without seeing its entire trajectory. If you know:

Current Position
f(a)

Order 0 approximation: you predict it stays where it is.

Speed & Heading
f'(a)

Order 1 approximation: straight tangent line projection.

Curve & Acceleration
f''(a) / 2!

Order 2 approximation: bends the projection into a parabola.
