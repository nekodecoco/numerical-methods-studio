Secant Method Superlinear (φ ≈ 1.618)

Derivative-Free Secant Projection

Replaces the analytical derivative with a finite-difference slope between two previous estimates:

  • Golden Ratio Rate: Faster than linear bisection, close to quadratic Newton-Raphson.
  • Efficiency: Requires only one new function evaluation per step after initial guesses!
  • No bracketing requirement (unlike False Position), but can diverge if initial points are poorly chosen.
