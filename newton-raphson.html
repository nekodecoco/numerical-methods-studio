Newton-Raphson Method

By fitting a tangent line with slope f'(x_i) at (x_i, f(x_i)), the next root estimate is where this line cuts the x-axis: x_{i+1} = x_i - f(x_i)/f'(x_i). Once near a simple root, the number of accurate decimal digits approximately doubles every iteration!

📖 Newton-Raphson Recipe

  4. Check Error: ε_a = |(x_{i+1} - x_i) / x_{i+1}| · 100%.
